My first day at SQI college of ICT

Starting My Web development Journey

Classes officially started on September 7th, I was quite anxious and nervous. there was more energy around, new faces and people who clearly already knew each other,I met few of my friends we were in same department at school. Atleast I have somebody to talk to.

settling In

The first day was more about orientation, as a web developer the things u need to know and the material which will aid proper learning and what the coming weeks would actually look like. I tried to listen as much as I could, as the lecturer passing some important information. I was surrounded by other people who also chose this path. This wasn't just about payment anymore. It was really happening.

Environment captivation

The college structure fascinated me as practical rather than been flashy and impressive. It had the feeling of a place where people came to work, not just attend classes. I also noticed, almost immediately, the range of people around me. Some clearly already had some coding background I could tell from how some of them casually they talked about things like HTML or javascript and so on. Unlike me, were coming in with more curiosity than experience.

Classes so far

Since September 7th,class started officially we've been steadily working through HTML and we're still on it. There's been no rush to move on before the foundation is solid, which I've come to appreciate that fact. Every session builds a little more on the last: new tags, new ways to structure a web page, small exercises and assignments to practice what we've learnt. It's the kind of pace that lets things actually sink in rather than just being rushed past. One thing that stands out about how the class runs is the lecturer's style She likes to ask questions constantly. Rather than just lecturing straight through, she'll pause and throw a question at the class to check who's actually following and listening to what she was saying. It keeps everyone a bit more alert, honestly, because you never quite know when you'll be the one answering next. It's pushed me to pay closer attention and actually think through the material instead of passively absorbing

So far, I can't point to any specific problem concerning class so far. HTML has been quite intresting maybe because we are still at the foundational level the pace has been fair, and nothing has thrown me off balance yet. I know that it would get more complicated as we advance, but for now, it feels like a steady, well-paced start.

One of the best parts of these first week has been the people. I've made more friend since class started The kind of easy friendships that form naturally when you're all sitting through the same lessons, asking the lecturer the same kinds of questions, and figuring out HTML together. There's a comfort in knowing I'm not navigating this alone. Where I'm At Now

Looking back from paying my fees on August 20th, to that first real day of class on September 7th, to now, still basics in HTML I notice how much has settled since the uncertainty at the beginning. There's no major problem to report so far,no obstacle and no sacriface yet I've had to push through, just steady, consistent learning: tag by tag, lesson by lesson, question by question from a lecturer who won't let anyone coast through a class unnoticed.

I know HTML is only the starting point, and that CSS, JavaScript, and everything beyond will bring their own challenges. But for now, with new friend beside me and a good rhythm to the classes. I feel like I'm exactly where I'm supposed to be at this stage of the journey.
